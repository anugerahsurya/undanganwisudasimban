### Mobile Layout Verification (R-03 & antislop-layoutmobile Checklist)

1. **Reflow & Hierarchy (R-03 PASS)**: Header, stat cards, upload dropzone, manual input form, and guest table collapse cleanly into mobile-tailored views.
2. **Zero Horizontal Leak (R-03 PASS)**: On screen widths < 768px, the wide table transforms into individual reflowed guest cards, eliminating clumsy horizontal table scroll on phones.
3. **Touch Targets (R-03 PASS)**: All interactive buttons (`btn-nav`, `btn-template`, `btn-gold-sm`, `btn-copy`, `btn-copy-link`, `btn-view`, `btn-delete`) have touch targets $\ge 42-44\text{ px}$.
4. **iOS Form Usability (R-03 PASS)**: Form inputs enforce 16px font-size on mobile viewports to prevent iOS Safari auto-zoom disruption.
5. **Stats Card 2x2 Grid (R-03 & R-05 PASS)**: Four stat cards reflow into a clean 2x2 grid on mobile viewports instead of 4 giant stacked cards.
